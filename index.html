Method and references
  • Planar: |αj − αf| ≤ lateral limit and φ < ψj < ψf (Markland 1972; Hoek & Bray 1981).
  • Wedge: line of intersection daylights, φ < ψi < apparent face dip along the trend; single-plane sliding flagged by the Hocking (1976) test. FS = K·tanφ / tanψi, K = sinβ / sin(ξ/2).
  • Flexural toppling: |αj − (αf+180°)| ≤ 10° and ψj ≥ (90° − ψf) + φ (Goodman & Bray 1976).
  • Fisher (1953) mean, k = (N−1)/(N−R), α95 = acos[1 − (N−R)/R·(201/(N−1) − 1)]. Density as multiples of uniform with an exponential kernel.

Method and references
  • Bieniawski, Z.T. (1989). Engineering Rock Mass Classifications. Wiley. RMR89 = A1 + A2 + A3 + A4 + A5.
  • Palmström, A. (2005). RQD from volumetric joint count: RQD = 110 − 2.5 Jv.
  • Romana, M. (1985). SMR = RMRb + F1·F2·F3 + F4. Tomás, R. et al. (2007) continuous F1, F2, F3.
Slopes

Plane failure factor of safety

Limit equilibrium for a block sliding on one plane, with a tension crack in the upper surface, water in the crack and on the plane, and a pseudo-static seismic coefficient (Hoek & Bray 1981; Wyllie & Mah 2004).

Geometry and strength

Leave crack depth blank to use the critical depth zc = H(1 − √(cotψf·tanψp)). For kh, half the IS 1893 zone factor (Z/2) is a common starting value.

Mining plans

Quarry reserves and life of mine

A first-pass reserve estimate for a minor-mineral or small mine lease: barrier and pit-slope losses, overburden, recovery, tonnes, m³ and brass, and the years of production the lease supports.

Lease and deposit

Benches

Barrier width, bench dimensions and the reserve categories you report must follow your state's Minor Mineral Concession Rules, the MCDR and IBM guidelines. Treat this as a working estimate for the mining plan, not the statutory document.

Reserves

Pit section

Volume between two surveys (grid of spot heights)

One grid point per line: RL before, RL after (optionally with x, y in front). Each point stands for one grid cell. Use it to check monthly despatch or royalty volumes against the pit survey.

Engineering

IS 1893 seismic coefficient

Design horizontal seismic coefficient Ah = (Z/2)·(Sa/g)·(I/R) by the equivalent static method of IS 1893 (Part 1):2016.

Which code applies. BIS issued IS 1893:2025 with a new Zone VI covering the Himalayan arc, then withdrew it by gazette notification on 3 March 2026. The 2016 edition remains in force, and this tool uses it. Check for later amendments before final design.
